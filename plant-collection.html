About This Database

This plant collection database is automatically updated from observations logged via iNaturalist. Each observation becomes an accession record in our formal collection, providing scientific documentation and tracking for Root Arboretum's living plant collection.

Data Quality: Observations must be identified plants (taxa rank: genus or species) to be included. This data supports our ArbNet Level 1 accreditation and helps us manage our 200-acre living collections.
