Trail Details

  • Distance: ~1.5 miles
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Surface: Stone trail
  • Time: ~1 hour

Key Features

  • Multiple vista points with panoramic views
  • Yatesville Creek overlook
  • Heritage garden area (Maryanne Granger plantings)
  • Historic Revolutionary War era site
  • Multiple rest areas with benches

Getting There

Address: 472 Anderson Road, Sprakers, NY 12166

Parking: Across the street in tennis/basketball court lot (~20 spaces)

What to Bring: Water, comfortable walking shoes, layers for weather

The Orboretum preserves, cultivates, and interprets native and heritage trees on a historically significant landscape in the Mohawk Valley.
